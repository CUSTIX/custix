Privacy policy

The N Place video uploader · Last updated November 18, 2026

What this app is

A personal tool that makes short videos about Negros Occidental and uploads them to The N Place YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel's owner. It is not offered to other users.

Google data it uses

  • It asks for one permission: upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • It does not read, collect, store or share any other Google account data: no email, contacts, viewing history, comments or analytics.
  • The sign-in token is kept only on the owner's computer and in the owner's private build settings, and is used only to upload the channel's own videos.

Viewers

The app collects nothing about people who watch the videos. YouTube's own privacy policy applies on YouTube.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the contact options at the developer's site.
